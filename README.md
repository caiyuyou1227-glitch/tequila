# TEQUILA / 01

### A PERSONAL WORKSPACE, ENGINEERED FOR FOCUS.

**Six tools. One native macOS experience. Your visual rules.**

Tequila is an open-source productivity workspace built with Swift, bringing task planning, time tracking, calendar visualization, email, and notes into one customizable environment.

Built around a simple idea: productivity software should work beautifully, not just function well.

## 00 / THE IDEA

### Less friction. More flow.

Most productivity tools are designed around isolated functions. Tequila takes a different approach: bring everyday workflows together while giving users control over how their workspace looks and feels.

Plan your day. Track your time. Visualize your schedule. Manage your inbox. Capture your ideas.

One workspace, shaped around the way you think.

---

## 01 / THE WORKSPACE

### Six modules. One system.

#### `01` FOCUS — Structure your day.

Turn tasks into a chronological plan. Organize time blocks for today or future dates, keep your schedule sorted automatically, and export your daily task list.

![Focus module](screenshots/focus.png)

#### `02` TIMER — Make time visible.

Track count-up and countdown sessions across unlimited projects. Organize timers into custom folders and understand where your time goes.

![Timer module](screenshots/timer.png)

#### `03` CALENDAR — Visualize your time.

Build a calendar around your own visual language. Layer highlighter colors, create schedule bars, customize fonts and colors, and export monthly schedules as PDFs in bar or circle styles.

![Calendar module](screenshots/calendar.png)

#### `04` MAIL — Your inbox, unified.

Bring multiple email accounts into one workflow. Tequila supports Gmail, Outlook, iCloud Mail, Proton Mail, Yahoo Mail, 163 Mail, and QQ Mail, with tools to read, manage, and reply to messages.

![Mail module](screenshots/mail.png)

#### `05` LOGS — A space for unfinished thoughts.

Keep notes, research, creative briefs, and project documentation in one organized workspace. Add images, format tables, and structure your ideas into folders and files.

![Logs module](screenshots/logs.png)

#### `06` SETTINGS — Make it yours.

Customize backgrounds with solid colors or images. Choose your typography and tailor the interface to your own aesthetic. Built-in localization helps make the experience accessible across languages.

![Settings module](screenshots/settings.png)

> **Designed to adapt.** Each module includes a movable location indicator that automatically adapts to regional settings.

## 02 / DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

### Function is the foundation. Design is part of the system.

Tequila explores the intersection of software engineering and visual design.

* **Native by design** — Built for macOS with Swift, AppKit, and SwiftUI.
* **Visual freedom** — Personalize backgrounds, typography, and interface colors.
* **Integrated workflows** — Move between planning, tracking, communication, and documentation in one app.
* **Open development** — Explore the source code, report issues, and help shape future iterations.

The goal isn't to add more features for the sake of it. It's to make everyday work feel more coherent.

## 03 / GET STARTED

### Your workspace, one download away.

**01 — Download**

Visit the [latest GitHub Release](../../releases/latest) and download `tequila.dmg`.

**02 — Install**

Open the DMG and drag Tequila into your Applications folder, if the installer provides this layout.

**03 — Launch**

Open Tequila from Applications.

Because the app may not be signed and notarized with an Apple Developer certificate, macOS may display a security warning. Only proceed if you've obtained the app from this official repository and are comfortable running it. If macOS blocks the app, consult Apple's current guidance for safely opening apps from unidentified developers.

**04 — Make it yours**

Open Settings to personalize the appearance and configure your workspace.

> Prefer to build from source? Clone this repository and open `tequila.xcodeproj` in Xcode.

## 05 / THE MAKER

### Built from a different perspective.

I'm a high school student with a background in fashion design, fashion management, and 2D/3D visual production.

Tequila began with a problem I experienced firsthand: productivity tools often made me choose between functionality and visual quality. I wanted a workspace that could support complex everyday tasks without giving up the freedom to shape its appearance.

So I started building one.

Developing Tequila has become an ongoing exploration of interface design, native macOS development, and the process of turning a personal tool into an open-source product.

It's a work in progress — and that's part of the point.
